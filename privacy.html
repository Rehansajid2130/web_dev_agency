Section 01

Data Controller Identification

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data collected through the digital properties of bit3labs Inc. ("bit3labs", "we", "us", or "our"). For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR 2016/679) and UK Data Protection Act 2018, bit3labs acts as the Data Controller.

Registered Entity: bit3labs Inc.
Data Protection Contact: privacy@bit3labs.com
Supervisory Scope: Global operations with dedicated compliance frameworks for EEA, UK, US, and Swiss residents.
Section 02

Categories of Information We Collect

We strictly adhere to the principle of Data Minimization (GDPR Article 5(1)(c)). We only collect personal data directly necessary to provide architectural feasibility consultations and technical contract scoping:

Category Data Elements Source
Identity & Contact Full Name, Work Email Address, Job Title / Role Directly submitted via Consultation Form
Organization & Scope Company / Brand Name, Project Domain (Web, Mobile, ERP), Timeline Directly submitted via Consultation Form
Technical Requirements Architecture specifications, legacy stack migration notes, user scale estimates Directly submitted via Technical Prompt Textarea
Technical & Telemetry IP Address (anonymized server logs), Browser Type, OS Automated HTTP server access logging

We do not collect sensitive categories of data (biometric data, racial origin, political opinions, or health records).

Section 03

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

Our processing activities are strictly justified under one or more recognized legal bases:

  • Performance of Contract / Pre-contractual Steps (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing your project specifications and scheduling senior engineering consultations requested by you.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Maintaining system stability, preventing denial of service or traversal attacks, and authenticating client inquiries.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): Meeting tax, corporate, accounting, and cross-border compliance obligations.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Explicit affirmative opt-in for receiving architecture whitepapers or engineering case study releases.
Section 04

Purpose of Processing & Non-Sale Commitment

Personal and technical data submitted through bit3labs is utilized exclusively for:

  1. Evaluating system scalability, technical feasibility, and architectural requirements.
  2. Preparing non-binding architectural roadmaps and consultation meeting agendas.
  3. Executing mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to code repository audits.
Zero Sale or Commercial Brokering of Data: bit3labs has never sold, rented, or leased client or prospect data to data brokers or advertising exchanges, and will never do so (complying with CCPA Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.120).
Section 05

Data Retention Schedules

We retain personal data only for the period necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which it was gathered:

  • Inquiry Consultation Data: Retained for 24 months from the date of last direct communication if no commercial Master Services Agreement (MSA) is executed, after which it is permanently purged.
  • Executed Project / Client Data: Retained for the statutory limitation period (7 years) pursuant to commercial record-keeping and tax legislation.
  • Server Security Logs: Retained for 30 days for intrusion detection and firewall auditing, after which rolling logs are overwritten.
Section 06

Data Subject Rights (EEA & UK Residents)

Under Articles 15 through 22 of the GDPR, European and UK residents possess statutory enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed and receive a structured export copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Demand correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact or architectural records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", Art. 17): Request deletion of your personal data where retention is no longer legally justified.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18): Restrict processing during dispute or verification periods.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your technical records in machine-readable JSON format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing predicated on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@bit3labs.com with the subject line "GDPR Data Request". We verify identity and respond within thirty (30) calendar days free of charge.

Section 07

California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act:

  • Notice at Collection: The categories of personal information collected and the specific purposes for which they are used are enumerated in Section 02 above.
  • Right to Know & Delete: California residents may request disclosure of categories of data collected and demand deletion without penalty.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not offer differential pricing, degraded response times, or altered engineering consultation services based upon the exercise of statutory privacy rights.
Section 08

Cookies & Client-Side LocalStorage

bit3labs does not employ intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or tracking beacons. We utilize strictly functional browser localStorage solely to preserve user interface state:

  • theme: Preserves user preference for Dark or Light interface modes.

Because these tokens are strictly necessary for client-side rendering preferences and do not transmit identifiable behavioral profiling data, they are exempt from cookie consent walls under ePrivacy Directive Article 5(3).

Section 09

Technical & Organizational Security Safeguards (GDPR Art. 32)

We implement comprehensive technical defenses to secure data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure:

  • Transport Security: Strict HTTPS transport encryption enforcing TLS 1.3 with HSTS flags.
  • Server Hardening: File serving containment preventing path traversal vulnerabilities; strict MIME validation; HTTP defensive security headers (X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN).
  • Confidential Architecture Reviews: Technical requirements submitted via consultation forms are stored in isolated encrypted data stores accessible only to authorized lead architects.
