Enterprise Database Architecture & Optimization

PostgreSQL schema engineering, query tuning, read-replica clustering, and zero-loss migrations.

Data is your most valuable asset. Bad indexing, unnormalized tables, and sloppy ORM queries silently degrade performance until user journeys stall. We design relational schemas with partial indexes, partitioning, read-replicas, and connection poolers (PgBouncer) that comfortably handle millions of rows.
